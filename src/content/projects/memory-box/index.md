---
title: "Memory Box"
summary: "A spaced-repetition app for keeping hold of the things I learn."
# live: https://example.com
date: 2026-08-13
---

I learn something, use it once, and then it's gone. Memory Box is meant to keep topics building up over years. You paste in raw material and an LLM drafts the cards. You answer in your own words and it grades you. The FSRS algorithm picks which cards are most at risk. Each day gets a fixed budget, so a week off never leaves you with a pile to catch up on.
