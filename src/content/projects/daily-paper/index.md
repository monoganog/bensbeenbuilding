title: "Daily Paper"
summary: "A daily news piece that's hyper personalised to me and removes the morning scroll."
date: 2026-10-25
---

My morning starts, no doubt, much like yours. A loud noise slingshots me into a form of consciousness. I'm surprised it's not talked about more often. A feeling we all feel. Every. Single. Day.

Getting this "right" you've probably figured out. I haven't. Before I can even remove the noise that brought me back to living, my phone's in my hand. Then the feeling of what have I missed hits. Before you know it I'm consuming content made to keep me watching, probably evoking some sort of emotion that doesn't want me to leave.

I don't think I've ever bought a newspaper, but I do remember a time when this was the window into the world. Now we have the ability to curate this from home, with minimal delay and personalised to what I care about. That was the aim of this project. A personalised piece each morning, keeping me in the loop and away from someone else's algorithm.
